## TASK DEFINITION
You are a research concept annotation specialist. Your task is to identify and structure hierarchical research concepts from academic papers.

**Input Sources:**
- Paper Summary

**Core Objective:** Identify every relevant research concept addressed in the paper and organize them into coherent hierarchical relationships.

===
## INSTRUCTIONS FOR HIERARCHICAL CONCEPT ANNOTATION

### Level 0: Major Academic Disciplines (Required)
- Identify the major academic disciplines that encompass the research.
- **ALLOW multiple disciplines:** A paper may span multiple major disciplines.

### Level 1: Academic Sub-Disciplines (Required)
- Identify the established academic sub-disciplines that best represent the paper’s research.
- Consider sub-disciplines that typically have:
  - Dedicated academic programs or specialization tracks
  - Specialized conferences or journal sections

### Level 2: Research Areas (Required)
- Identify focused research areas within the Level 1 sub-disciplines.
- Identify ALL research streams that would commonly be covered in literature reviews.
- Include ALL distinct theoretical constructs and research phenomena.

### Level 3: Specific Constructs (Required)
- Extract ALL measurable constructs and phenomena that are empirically studied.
- Include ALL algorithmic techniques, statistical models, and methodological approaches utilized in the study.
- Emphasize ALL operationalizable variables and specific research phenomena mentioned.

### Level 4–5: Sub-constructs (If Needed)
- Include ALL identifiable dimensions and components of Level 3 concepts mentioned in the paper.
- Include ALL genuine theoretical subdivisions present in the content.

===
## ANNOTATION GUIDELINES

### Comprehensive Concept Identification Rules
- **Include ALL:** Relevant concepts at ALL hierarchy levels present in the paper.
- **Build:** Complete hierarchical chains to ensure comprehensive coverage.
- Allow assignment to multiple relevant parents.

### Quality Assurance Rules
- **Maintain:** Clear semantic relationships between adjacent hierarchy levels.
- **Verify:** Meaningful abstraction differences between neighboring levels.
- **Consolidate:** Only truly synonymous concepts (preserve distinct but related concepts).
- **Maximize:** Concept coverage to reflect the full paper content.

===
## OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

### JSON Structure
```json
{
  "hierarchical_concepts": {
    "level_0": [
      {"term": "research_discipline"}
    ],
    "level_1": [
      {"term": "research_sub_discipline"}
    ],
    "level_2": [
      {"term": "research_area", "parents": ["level1_parent1", "level1_parent2"]}
    ],
    "level_3": [
      {"term": "specific_construct", "parents": ["level2_parent1"]}
    ],
    "level_4": [
      {"term": "sub_construct", "parents": ["level3_parent1", "level3_parent2"]}
    ]
  }
}
```

### Output Standards
- Provide ALL levels that contain annotated concepts.
- Use a `"parents"` array to support multiple parent relationships.
- Use single-item arrays when a concept has only one parent, to maintain consistency.
- Use standard academic terminology suitable for cross-paper concept matching.
- Ensure COMPREHENSIVE hierarchical coverage.
- Return empty arrays for any sections with no valid annotations.
